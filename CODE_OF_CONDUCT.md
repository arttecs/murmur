# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences;
- accepting constructive criticism gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the project;
- showing empathy towards other community members.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and discussions, and when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at [security@arttecs.com](mailto:security@arttecs.com?subject=%5BMURMUR%5D%20Conduct) with a subject that starts with `[MURMUR]`. Reports are reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
